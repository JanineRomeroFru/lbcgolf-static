About

LBC Golf is the hub for Long Beach golf course and home to some of the best and most affordable public golf facilities in Southern California. In addition to outstanding facilities and world-class service, Long Beach golf is dedicated to helping people of all ages and backgrounds learn and enjoy the game of golf.

Of equal importance is our commitment to improve the quality of life for those who live and work in the communities we serve. That's why we support local grass roots charitable efforts and initiate player development programs aimed at increasing opportunities for juniors and adults of all backgrounds to learn and play the game of golf.

In addition, to strengthen the company's continued efforts to enhance and enrich the communities we serve, American Golf assisted in establishing the American Golf Foundation (AGF), a non-profit organization dedicated to promoting the game of golf and its ideals through charity, education and service to the community.
