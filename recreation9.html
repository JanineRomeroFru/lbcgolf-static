About Recreation Park 9 Golf Course:

Recreation Park 9, otherwise known as "Little Rec," is located directly across 7th Street, from the majestic 18-hole course. This nine-hole executive golf facility affords guests a picturesque setting which range from rolling hills to views of the Colorado Lagoon. Rec Park 9 Golf Course also offers a great place to strengthen your game with challenging greens and strategically placed bunkers.

View Larger Map

Amenities:

  • Cart Rental: Electric carts are available upon request.
  • Chipping Green(s): Multiple practice chipping greens are available for the refining of your short game.
  • Onsite Pro: Staffed with PGA and LPGA professionals we have individual and group lessons available.
  • Pro Shop: Our Pro Shop carries the latest collection in golf apparel, equipment and accessories. We open 30 minutes before sunlight and close 30 minutes after sunset.
  • Pull Cart: Pull Carts are available upon request.
  • Putting Green(s): An over sized practice green is available to develop your putting.
  • Snack Bar: Adjacent to the Pro Shop is our Snack Bar. The perfect place for ice cold beverages before your round or a great place to get a bite to eat after you have played. In addition there is a BBQ Grill on the 6th Tee
