LBCGolf.com offers the best in Long Beach golf

Advanced tee time reservations are available online for all golfers and additional booking benefits are available specifically for Long Beach city residents.

With a number of recent renovations, Skylinks at Long Beach offers a great course layout featuring over 70 sand bunkers across its 18 holes.  Nearby is El Dorado Park Golf Course, a venue sought out by many local golfers due to its playability for novice and experienced golfers alike.

Just down the street, adjacent to Cal State Long Beach, you will find Recreation Park 18, which is one of the oldest public golf courses in Southern California. Initially opened in 1929 this golf course provides rolling hills and a variety of hole layouts.


The sister course to Recreation Park 18 is Recreation Park 9. This is one of two executive golf courses featured at LBCGolf.com and is ideal for fine tuning one's short game. The other featured executive public golf course in Long Beach is Heartwell Golf Course, located a drivers distance from the Long Beach Civic Center. This par 54 course is perfect for a beginning golfer or anybody looking to get out for a quick round of golf.

Make your next tee time online at LBCGolf.com with any of these five Long Beach golf courses and enjoy all that Long Beach golf has to offer. LBCGolf.com is the premier destination for locals to find golf tee times.
