Instruction

Looking to fine tune your swing or just learn how to play?

LBCgolf.com courses offer numerous ways to work on your game. A variety of lesson options are available including: one-day clinics, individual lessons, group lessons, video lessons, and playing lessons.

Juniors

LBCgolf.com courses offer tailored instruction for juniors to ensure learning is effective and fun. The Long Beach Junior Golf Academy is a year round golf league and training program for players age 5-16.

Little Linksters is a city sponsored summer camp held every summer. This program is available for 2 weeks at a time all summer long and is open to players age 5-16. Course sponsored summer camps and clinics are also available.
