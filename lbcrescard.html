LBC Res. Card

LBCgolf.com courses accept the LBC Resident and Reservations Cards. In order to make advanced golf reservations, you must purchase an advance reservation card. However, possession of this card does not guarantee a reservation. Cards are available for purchase at Recreation Park 18.

Both the Resident and Reservation card are good for one (1) year from the date of purchase. To be eligible to purchase a Long Beach Resident card, a valid Driver's license with a Long Beach address and an accompanying current utility bill (Gas, Power, Water, Trash) with the same address and name is required. Cards are non-transferable and non-refundable.


Reservation Policies

  • No reservation will be granted without a valid Resident card.
  • To get the resident or senior resident discount rate you must present the resident card to the starter, otherwise he/she will pay the non-resident rate.
  • If a reservation is made under your number and name and someone else shows up for the tee times with your card, you will be the one to forfeit the reservation.
  • Cardholders must check in at least 20 minutes prior to their starting time or the reserved time will be given to a player from the waiting list. The cardholder will then be given the 1st available tee time.
  • Please note: Holders of reservation cards are subject to such suspensions and restrictions as American Golf may consider reasonable for failure to comply with the rules and regulations governing etiquette of the game, rules of play and personal conduct.
