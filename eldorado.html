About El Dorado Park Golf Course:

El Dorado Park Golf Course is a magnificent 18 hole layout that can truly be called a "player's course," due to its expansive layout and numerous water hazards. El Dorado Park Golf Course offers extensive practice facilities and a full service restaurant. In addition, the course was the previous venue of the Queen Mary Open, and is now the site of the prestigious Long Beach Open.
