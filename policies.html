Policies

Advanced Reservation Policies for The City of Long Beach

  1. In order to make advanced golf reservations up to 8 days in advance, you must purchase an advance reservation/ photo ID card. However, possession of this card does not guarantee a reservation. Please have your card number ready when you go online for reservations.
  2. Cardholders must check in at least 20 minutes before their starting time or the time will be cancelled and players from the waiting list will be assigned that starting time.
  3. Reservations are available at www.lbcgolf.com up to 8 days in advance. Residents may make online reservations at 6am with their membership card number. Non resident reservation card members may make online reservations up to 8 days in advance beginning at 6:30am with their reservation card number. All public may make online reservations up to 7 days in advance starting at 6am. Reservations are only being accepted online at this time.
  4. OBSERVED HOLIDAYS

    Reservations for holidays will be taken eight days in advance just like for all other days. The following are observed holidays for Long Beach golf courses:

    • New Year's Day
    • Martin Luther King, Jr. Day
    • President's Day
    • Good Friday
    • Memorial Day
    • Juneteenth
    • July 4th
    • Labor Day
    • Election Day
    • Veteran's Day
    • Thanksgiving Day
    • Friday after Thanksgiving
    • Christmas Day


    NOTE: For any holidays falling on a weekend, the National Day of Observance will be holiday rates. If July 4th, Christmas Day or New Year's Day falls on a Tuesday or a Thursday, then the adjacent Monday or Friday will be considered a holiday for rate purposes. Retired or active military with valid ID shall receive 50% off of the green fee they would otherwise pay (resident or non-resident) on Veteran's Day (rev. 8/2014).
  5. To get the resident or senior resident discount rate you must show the golf discount/reservation card to the starter, otherwise he/she will pay the non-resident rate.
  6. Only one (1) starting time allowed per day per card number. If more than one tee time appears on the same day under the same card number both tee times will be cancelled.
  7. Cards are not transferable. If a reservation is made under your number and name and someone else shows up for the tee times with your card, you will be the one to forfeit the privileges. Holders of reservation cards are subject to such suspensions and restrictions as American Golf may consider reasonable for failure to comply with the rules and regulations governing etiquette of the game, rules of play and personal conduct.
  8. The City of Long Beach Department of Parks, Recreation & Marine rules and regulations do not allow food or beverages of any kind to be brought onto/or consumed on the course. All food and beverages must be purchased from the course concessionaire
  9. Your reservation card is good for one year. It must be renewed by the end of the month that appears on the front of your card. Long Bach Residents must still bring a valid Driver's License along with a utility bill. The address on the two must have the same Long Beach address.


Reservation Policy

When reserving a tee time on Friday through Sunday you will be asked to guarantee your reservation by providing a valid credit card number.

Cancellation Policy

To cancel your reservation you must call the Pro Shop at the course at least 48 hours before your scheduled tee time. If you do not cancel your reservation 48 hours prior to your time, you will be charged a cancellation fee of $20 per player. The cancellation fee will be charged to the credit card you provided at the time you made the reservation.

If you have any questions please call 562-494-5000.
